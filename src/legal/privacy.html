{{BRAND}} gives traditional Indian palmistry interpretations of a photograph of your palm, for reflection and entertainment. This page explains, item by item, what personal data the app uses, why, where it goes, and how you remove it. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Google Play's User Data policy. The rules for using the app and buying readings are in the terms of use.

The short version

  • No server keeps your palm photo. It is analysed once, by Cloudflare Workers AI and by our line scanner on Modal (USA), through our own server. A small copy stays only on your phone, inside the app, so your report can show the lines on your own palm — until you delete that reading.
  • We keep only the written observations and the reading they produced, so your history works.
  • Your name, age, city and birth time stay only on your phone.
  • No advertising, no selling of data, no tracking across other apps.
  • You can delete your readings, or your whole account, at any time — in the app or on the web.

We never receive your card or UPI details. We do not infer health, ethnicity or any other sensitive trait from your hand, and readings never give medical, financial or legal advice.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase — database, sign-in, the one-time email code, and server functions.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI — analyses the palm photo and returns written observations, then writes the report text from those observations (no photo is sent for that); nothing is kept.
  • Modal (USA) — runs our palm-line scanner. It receives the palm photo from our server, returns the traced lines, and keeps nothing.
  • Google Play — app distribution and payments.

They act on our instructions, only for the purposes above. Some of this processing happens outside India, for example the line scanner in the USA.

Your consent and your rights

Before your first reading the app asks for your explicit consent to analyse the photo. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data or account. You have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, and to nominate another person to exercise these rights.

  • Delete readings: in the app, Profile → Delete account readings.
  • Delete your account: in the app, Profile → Delete my account, or on the web at delete-account.html, without reinstalling the app.

Deleting your account removes it and everything linked to it on our side. Done in the app, it also removes from that phone your readings, profile, the saved descriptions of recent palm photos and any report files you shared. We keep only the one-way email code and, for up to 2 days, the one-way internet-address code described above. If our server cannot be reached, the app finishes the deletion the next time you open it with internet.

Age

{{BRAND}} is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Each account can only ever read its own readings, enforced by the database itself. Secret keys never ship inside the app.
