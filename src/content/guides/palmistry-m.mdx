## What forms the M on your palm?

The M is made by four lines you already have. The heart line runs across the top of the palm, and the head line runs below it. The life line curves round the ball of the thumb.

The fourth is the fate line, which runs up the middle of the palm towards the middle finger. Where it crosses the head and heart lines, the four lines can look like a capital M. People call it the letter M on the palm, the M sign or, in Hindi, <Hi>हाथ में M का निशान</Hi> (hath me M ka nishan).

## Is the M on your palm rare?

We can’t say, and neither can anyone we could cite. No study and no classical palmistry book we use counts how many palms show an M. Claims online that only a small share of people have one give no source, so we don’t repeat a number.

What we can say is why the M varies. Most hands show a heart, head and life line. The exception is a hand with one crease across the palm instead of two, the [simian line](/simian-line/). The fate line is the one most often faint, short or missing, so it usually decides whether your M is full.

## What does the M on your palm mean?

In the classical books, nothing on its own. We checked the palmistry books the PalmSays app is built on, from Cheiro’s books to Mrs Dale’s *Indian Palmistry* (1895). None of them describes an M sign.

Online you may read that an M means intuition, leadership, luck or wealth. Those claims are modern and come with no source. Read them as belief, not tradition. The honest way to read an M is line by line.

### The heart line in an M

The top stroke. Palmistry reads it for feelings and how you relate to other people. Its shape and where it ends are explained in [heart line meaning](/heart-line/).

### The head line in an M

The stroke across the middle. It is read for how you think and decide: a straight line as practical, a sloping one as imaginative. See [head line meaning](/head-line/).

### The life line in an M

The curve round the thumb. It is read for energy and how you meet change. Its length is never read as years: see [life line meaning](/life-line/).

### The fate line in an M

The line up the middle that closes the M. It is read for direction and work, and a faint or missing fate line is common. See [fate line meaning](/fate-line/).

## M on the left hand, right hand or both hands

The M is read the same way on either hand, for women and men alike. Tradition reads your writing hand as the life you are making and the other hand as what you started with.

An M on both hands means the four lines meet the same way on each. An M on one hand only is ordinary, because your two hands are rarely the same. No book gives either case a meaning of its own. The guide to [which hand to read](/which-hand-to-read/) explains the traditions.

## Can a phone photo show the M?

Usually, yes. The M is made of the four major lines, and those are big enough for a phone photo. Small marks such as crosses and stars are not: they are only a few pixels wide. The PalmSays scanner traces exactly these four lines.

The weak point is a faint fate line. Flat light can hide it, and then your M looks broken when it isn’t. Take the photo in bright, even daylight, with your palm flat and your fingers together.

## Myths about the M on the palm

<MythReality>
<Myth claim="An M on your palm is a sign of wealth or luck.">

No classical palmistry book says so. The M is four ordinary lines, and no line predicts money or luck.

</Myth>
<Myth claim="Only a few people in the world have an M.">

No study counts it. Most palms show a heart, head and life line; a full M on yours depends mostly on the fate line.

</Myth>
<Myth claim="An M shows a gift for reading people or seeing the future.">

That is a modern belief with no source in the books. The nearest classical reading is the mystic cross, read by Cheiro as an interest in the spiritual. It is a different mark, and it is still not a power.

</Myth>
</MythReality>
